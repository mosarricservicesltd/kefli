# Naming: Kefli

Decided by Richard on 9 October 2026. Closes mobile spec O-9 for the name; the app ID, domains and the trade mark filing are the follow-ups in the last section.

## The name

**Kefli**, from Old Norse and Icelandic *kefli*, a short wooden stick. *Rúnakefli* are rune sticks: everyday messages carved into wood so they could be carried and kept. The Norse connotations are the brand identity.

- **Said in English as "KEF-lee".** The Icelandic sound is closer to "KYEP-lih" (between vowels Icelandic *fl* is spoken [pl]); the house pronunciation is the English one, and marketing copy doesn't claim "KEV-lee".
- **Why it fits.** A rune stick is a note made to outlast the hand that carved it, which is the pitch: the library outlives the service. Hundreds of medieval rune sticks were dug up at Bryggen in Bergen after the 1955 fire, most of them ordinary notes and labels, still legible some 800 years on (check the figures before they go in copy). The *Hávamál* gives a line for the same idea: *deyr fé, deyja frændr … en orðstírr deyr aldregi*, "cattle die, kinsmen die … but the good name never dies".
- **It fits the icon.** Decision 94's mark is an upright, notched bookmark, a cut stick in its own right; nothing in the icon changes for the name (decision 67 froze it so that a rename wouldn't).
- **Store name.** Kefli alone where the store allows, with a descriptor where search needs one ("Kefli: read it later").

## The checks (9 October 2026)

Deep dive on Kefli, scored 6 out of 10 against the other finalists. V is checked directly against a register, store or registry; S is from search summaries.

- **Trade marks: no blocker.** No live KEFLI mark in classes 9, 16, 35, 38, 41 or 42 in the UK, EU, US, WIPO, Iceland, the Nordics or Turkey (V: TMview and the USPTO search). The only exact mark, Turkish "kefli şekil" (class 38), has ended. KEFLEX (cefalexin, Eli Lilly and Flynn Pharma) is class 5 only. A UK filing in classes 9 and 42 looks clean.
- **Stores.** No Kefli app on Google Play, the App Store, the Chrome Web Store, Firefox add-ons or F-Droid (V). The Play IDs `app.kefli`, `com.kefli`, `com.kefli.app`, `ink.kefli` and `io.kefli` are all unused (V). One small Play developer, "Kefli Games" (Israel, one game, 1k+ downloads), uses the name as a publisher (V).
- **Domains.** Unregistered on the day: kefli.app, .io, .co, .dev, .org, .uk, .co.uk, .is, getkefli.app, getkefli.com, kefliapp.com, readkefli.com (V). kefli.com is parked on Afternic with a GoDaddy for-sale listing, price unseen (V). kefli.ink is probably unregistered but the registry was rate-limiting (unconfirmed).
- **Handles.** `kefliapp` and `getkefli` are free on GitHub, Bluesky and X; `kefli` itself is taken (dormant) on GitHub and X and free on Bluesky (V). npm and PyPI `kefli` are free (V).

### Known baggage

- **kefli.net** was registered on 18 August 2026 and serves a Hebrew-language escort site (V). Never link or print .net; consider who reaches it by mistake.
- **Azerbaijani *kefli* means "drunk"** (V, Wiktionary). In Hebrew, *kef li* is roughly "I'm enjoying this" (S), which likely explains the Play developer and kefli.net.
- **Everyday Icelandic** uses *kefli* for a rolling pin, a spool, a relay baton and a gag (*kefla*, to gag) (V). Harmless, and mildly comic to Icelanders.
- **In the US the spelling echoes Keflex**, the antibiotic; much less so in the UK.
- **Spelling it back from hearing it** is unreliable (Kefly, Kefley, Keffly), so the descriptor and the domain carry it at first.
