Revision 2, September 2026, aligned to contract v1.1. Changed from revision 1: the browser extension never receives the signing key; backup is the phone's own keychain sync with the recovery code as an optional export; saves go to three relays so the library survives our server; the better voice is cached per article and served only to people who saved it, and never replaces the voice mid-article; "Play all" is the only auto-continue.

Decisions baked into the map

Three tabs: Saves, Listen, You. No Following tab in v1. Queue model is one Unread list in save order plus Archive; no tags, favourites or folders. No account, email or password: the key is created on the phone and backed up by iCloud Keychain or Google's backup automatically, with a standard 12-word recovery code available under You for anyone leaving Apple or Google. The browser extension has its own device key, approved once from the phone, and never holds the user's key; its saves reach the phone as sealed notes and become real saves when the phone next opens. Every save is encrypted and written to three relays, ours plus two independent ones, and article text is mirrored to a second storage server, so a new phone restores the whole library even if our servers are gone. Parsing happens on the device that saved. Listening starts instantly with the phone's own voice; a better voice is generated once per article on our server and offered only to people who saved that article. The word nostr appears once, on the "Sign in with your nostr app" button.
