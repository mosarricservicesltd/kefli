# Consistency spec (phone)

The house rules every Shelf screen follows, so the same role looks, sits and behaves the same everywhere. Produced on 2026-09-27 by a measured, verified consistency review of the phone prototype, then settled by Richard (decisions 45–54 in `flow-decisions.md`, which win where a rule below mentions an open question). New screens follow these rules; a deliberate exception needs a decision.

## The player

- **One listening session drives three tiers built from the same parts.** Compact (mini): every screen where audio is loaded, except Listen while its Now playing card is on screen, Speed read, first launch (welcome, return, scan, code, nostr), the lock screen, and the playing article's Reader. Reader tier: the playing article's Reader. Where it sits is Q1; the recommended answer is the mini expanded in place at the bottom. Full: the Listen Now playing card and Speed read's transport. The lock screen uses Android's media layout, and Shelf supplies the title, source, play/pause and ±15 s.
- **Every player uses one control order: back 15, play/pause, forward 15, speed, then ✕ while paused. Compact tiers put play/pause first; full tiers centre it.** Mini: [pp 48][title 13/600 over time 13][✕ 48 while paused], gap 12, padding 0 8, so pp sits at x=24. Reader tier: [pp 48][time, flex][−15 48][+15 48][speed ≥52][✕ 48 while paused], all gaps 4 and no extra pp margin. Now playing card: the controls row is a grid 1fr 48px 60px 48px 1fr with column-gap 8. −15, play and +15 go in columns 2–4 and the speed pill in column 5 with justify-self:end; column 5 is 54 px inside the 296 px card interior. No empty placeholder. Speed read: [previous sentence 48][play 60][next sentence 48], gap 24, with the wpm stepper below.
- **Two play/pause sizes and one gradient.** Play/pause is 48 in compact tiers and 60 with var(--shadow-float) in full tiers, and no other size. The gradient goes only on the live play/pause; whether the Reader's Listen chip keeps it is Q6. −15, +15 and ✕ are 48 hit areas with 24, 24 and 20 px icons. Entry points (Listen, Play all, a row's play button) are soft: accent-soft fill and an accent icon, as pills (36 visual, 48 hit) or circles (48).
- **One speed pill in players and one stepper for stepped values. The mini shows no speed.** Pill: 36 visual, 48 hit (::before inset −8px −2px), min-width 52, padding 0 12, 13/600 tabular. Fill is var(--background) on app surfaces and color-mix(in srgb, var(--reader-muted) 15%, transparent) with var(--reader-text) on reader surfaces. A tap steps through one list [0.8, 1, 1.2, 1.5, 2] and wraps from 2 back to 0.8. Stepper (Voice → Default speed, Speed read wpm): height 48, flush 48×48 −/+ buttons, value 15/600 tabular, min-width 48 and sized to its content ('1.5×', '300 wpm'). Fill is var(--background), or reader-muted 14% on reader surfaces.
- **Players show clock time and everything else shows 'N min left', all read from one stored position.** Mini and Reader tier: 'm:ss / m:ss' (elapsed / total) at 13/500 tabular in the surface's muted colour. When the Reader tier is paused and shows ✕, it shows elapsed only. Now playing card: elapsed at the left and total at the right under the bar, never '-m:ss'. Cards, Listen rows and Speed read's meta show 'N min left'. Each article has one stored position (a word index in the build, it.progress in the prototype). Reading, listening and Speed read all write it, and every bar, 'min left', player time and Speed read read it.
- **Bars only show progress. A knob appears only on a bar that can be dragged.** Pending Q4 (recommended: no seeking in v1), no bar has a knob. Mini line: 2 px, left/right 28, bottom 5. Reader reading line: 2 px under the top bar, hidden while this article's Reader tier is showing, so one position has one indicator. Player bars: 3 px. Speed read bar: 2 px. The Aa sheet's size track has no knob because the A buttons set the size.
- **Every player surface ends the session the same way.** While paused, each tier shows the same ✕ 'Close player' (.pclose: 48, 20 px x icon, muted colour) at its trailing end. On the mini and the Reader tier it goes at the right end of the row; on the Now playing card at the top right (absolute, top 4, right 4). Closing from any tier clears S.audio everywhere: the mini slides out, the Reader tier reverses out, the Listen chip and the reading line come back, and the Now playing card goes away.
- **The live player is anchored to the bottom. Only the Listen tab shows it as page content.** Mini at [16,624,328,64] on tab screens (bottom 72) and [16,680,328,64] on pushed screens and other articles' Readers (bottom 16). Playing article's Reader: Q1, recommended at the same [16,680,328,64], expanded. Listen: the Now playing card sits in the scroll, and whether the mini appears after it scrolls away is Q3. Speed read: transport in its bottom block. Tapping the mini's title (not its play button) opens the Reader at the spoken word.
- **Tapping an article opens it, and only play controls play. Pausing never ends Play all.** Saves and Search cards, the mini's title, the Now playing title and the lock-screen card all open the Reader. Listen rows are Q2; the recommended answer is that the row body opens the Reader and a leading 48 play button plays. Pausing or resuming the current article from any control leaves S.playAll and S.queue unchanged. Only starting a different article ends Play all.

## Screen scaffolding

- **One gutter.** --gutter:16px applies to tab screens, the pushed-screen body and action bar, sheets, the mini, the toast, notices and the Archive dock's right inset. --gutter-reader:20px applies only to the Reader's article column. The share sheet's countdown line keeps left/right 24 (= --radius-sheet) so it starts where the corner curve ends. Components inside .onbbody keep zero horizontal margin (.ygroup, .gnote, .banner, .gtitle) so the gutter is never doubled.
- **Tab screens (Saves, Listen, You) have no top bar, just a header row inside the scroll.** Header row padding 12 16 8. The title is 28/1.2, weight 600, at y=12 on the left; the count is 13 muted, right-aligned on the same row (Saves 'N unread', Listen 'N played', You 'N saves · N listened'). Nostr users get the profile block under the row and no count. Tab bar 64 at the bottom. Primary actions are inline: content width and centred in empty states, inline in cards. Tab screens never have a bottom action bar.
- **Pushed screens have a pinned 56 bar with ← at x=8, a large title, a bottom action bar and no tab bar.** ← is a 48 .iconbtn at x=8..56 (glyph x≈21). The large title is 28/1.2 at x=16, y=64. Once it scrolls under the bar, the title appears in the bar at 15/600, x=64, with a 150 ms fade. Body padding 8 16. Action bar padding 16 16 24, with full-width 48 buttons stacked 8 apart and the primary on top. Search uses this bar (56 tall, ← at x=8, field x=60..344 and 48 tall); whether it keeps the tab bar is Q8.
- **Back pops one step, and Cancel never disagrees with ←.** ← and Android Back return to the screen or state that started the current one, and a visible Cancel goes to the same place. States that can't be left (switch 'moving', delete 'running') hide ← and swallow Android Back. Pushed flows return to their launcher on Back and on success; Connect opened from Saves' first-run state returns to Saves. Reader ← goes to Saves (decision 33), except that a Reader opened from Search returns to Search with the query, results and scroll kept. The Listen and mini routes are Q7. A cold start straight into the Reader has no history, so ← goes to Saves.
- **The Reader and the full-screen modes carry the same article actions in the same places.** Reader bar (56): [← x=8][host centred][Listen chip][Aa 48][⋯ 48], with the 2 px reading line under it. Prose at 20, no tab bar. Archive, or Back to Unread for an archived article, floats 52×52 at right 16. The in-app browser view of a save gets bar [✕ x=8][title/host][⋯], where ⋯ holds Open in browser, Copy link and Delete, plus the same floating Archive. Speed read: a 56 bar with ✕ at x=8 that returns to the Reader at the current word, and the transport in the bottom block.
- **One bottom stack, built from the bottom up with 8 px between layers.** Order: tab bar or screen edge → mini or Reader dock → the screen's own bottom layer (action bar or floating Archive) → toast. Offsets from the viewport bottom: - Tab screens: mini 72; toast 72, or 144 with the mini. - Reader: Archive 16, or 88 above a mini or dock; toast 76, or 148. - Pushed screen without an action bar: mini 16; toast 16, or 88. - Pushed screen with an action bar: mini 16; bar bottom padding 24, or 88 with the mini; toast = bar height + 8 (96 for one button, 152 for two, 168 for Welcome; 160 and 216 with the mini). Every layer spans x=16..344 except Archive, which is right-aligned. Scroll content ends with bottom padding of at least the floating layers' height + 16 (156 in a Reader with a mini or dock).
- **Fixed z-order, and no toast over a sheet.** Content < Archive dock 3 < mini 20 < toast 30 < scrim and sheet 40 < Speed read 45 < lock screen 50 < system dialog 60. No toast is raised while a sheet is open. A sheet action either closes the sheet first (Copy link, Delete, Back to Unread, Disconnect) or confirms inside the sheet (recovery code Copy). A toast already showing when a sheet opens pauses under the scrim and resumes with its remaining time when the sheet closes. Sheets: padding 8 16 24, radius 24 on the top corners, 36×4 grab handle, and one horizontal button row with each button half width.
- **Each screen has one notice slot.** On pushed screens it is directly under the h1, before the lead. On tab screens it follows the header block: on Saves after the Unread/Archive segments, on You after the header row. When there are two causes, the error comes first. A component that already states the condition (the restore card's 'Paused…' line) replaces the notice.

## Components

- **One primary per screen. Pairs put the primary on the right; stacks put it on top.** Primary: gradient, 48 tall. Full width in pushed action bars, content width and centred in empty states, content width in cards, half width in sheets. Secondary: surface fill, same sizes. Quiet (accent text): only under a stacked primary. Danger (solid error): only for irreversible confirms. Horizontal pairs are [secondary][primary]. Card rows are centred in centred cards (end card) and right-aligned in left-aligned cards (backup card). A deferral paired in a row ('Later', 'Not now') is secondary. An error's recovery action ('Try again') is the primary.
- **Chips are 36 visual with a 48 hit area, and icon buttons are always 48.** Chips: segments, speed, suggestions, Listen, Play all, Paste and text buttons, each with a ::before inset of −8px −2px. ← appears on pushed screens, Search and the Reader. ✕ appears only on full-screen modes (Speed read, the in-app browser view) and on player surfaces.
- **One container scale.** Every card has radius 10 (var(--radius)), including Now playing. Radius 24 (var(--radius-sheet)) is only for sheets, the viewfinder and illustrations. Padding is 12 in list items, 16 in content cards (backup, restore, Now playing, end card, confirm card) and 24 only in the QR card.
- **Type roles.** 28/1.2: screen and article titles. 20/600: empty-state, sheet and completion headings, including both 'You've reached the end'. 17/600: card and section headings ('Played', 'Your twelve words', the restore and backup cards, the Now playing title). 15: body text and rows. 13/600 muted: group labels, with no uppercase or letter-spacing ('Relays', 'Now playing'). 13: meta. Nothing below 13 in Shelf's own UI; drawn system UI (share sheet, lock screen) is exempt.
- **One in-flow notice component in three tones. The inverted pill is only for transient floating messages.** Notice: radius 10, padding 12 16, a 16 px leading icon, 13/500 text in var(--text). Info: accent-soft fill with an accent icon; the in-app browser notice uses it too, dropping #F8EEEA. Error (errline): error at 10% over the surface, with an error icon. Offline: Q5, recommended neutral (var(--surface), 1px var(--line) border, muted wifioff icon). Field errors sit at the field: a 1px var(--error) border and a 13 px .fielderr directly under it, with the input kept.
- **Every empty state uses the same parts.** .medal: a 56 px accent-soft circle with a 26 px accent icon. Then an h2 at 20/600, one muted line (max 28ch) and at most one button. The two exceptions are the first-run share-sheet illustration and All read's green check. .check means success and nothing else.
- **Waiting states look the same everywhere.** .pdot is 8 px with a 1.2 s breathe and always starts the meta line. Three 10 px dots at 1.2 s appear only in the centred waiting block. Counted work (restore, switch) uses a determinate bar. Skeleton lines shimmer like .fig.shimmer. A busy button keeps full opacity, shows three dots and an '-ing' label, and sets aria-busy and pointer-events:none. It never uses [disabled].
- **One code-field component.** .codefield: 52 tall, pill-shaped, 1px var(--line) border, var(--surface) fill, centred 20/600 tabular text. Six digits use letter-spacing .35em and the numeric keypad. XXXX-XXXX uses .2em, uppercase, with the hyphen inserted automatically. The field moves on when complete. .bad adds a var(--error) border and a .fielderr under the field, and the value is kept and selected. Recovery-word fields (.wf) are 48 tall.
- **One icon per concept, and settings rows share weights.** archive; restore (Back to Unread); trash; laptop for any browser, including the From Chrome card; keyboard for the recovery code row and typed codes; shield for the backup card; login for nostr; headphones for Listen as a place; play for Listen as an action; wifioff for offline. yrow: bare muted icon, 500 label, sub-line, trailing state, 18 chevron. .choice keeps its 44 icon circle and 600 label but uses the 18 chevron.

## Interaction and feedback

- **Gates follow decision 14.** If this phone can reverse an action, it happens at once with an Undo toast (archive, back to unread, delete a save). If reversing it would need another device or a new pairing, it asks first in a confirm sheet laid out [Cancel][named consequence] (disconnect a browser, switch identity). Irreversible data loss gets an explain screen and a typed DELETE. Nothing else asks.
- **Toasts are for transient results only.** The toast is an inverted pill, min-height 52, placed by the bottom-stack rule. Durations: getRecommendedTimeoutMillis(5000) on the phone; the share sheet keeps rev. 3's 4 s via getRecommendedTimeoutMillis(4000); the extension keeps 8 s (decision 30). Nothing auto-dismisses while a screen reader is on. One 2 px accent countdown bar: full width along the toast's bottom edge, and along the share sheet's top edge inset 24. Timers pause on touch or focus and resume with the remaining time. Undo appears only when the action can be reversed. Copy is a past-tense result in sentence case, with no full stop and no 'we', about five words (names may run longer). A second sentence appears only when the user must act.
- **Undo reverses the whole action, including any navigation it caused.** A Reader action that navigated away (Archive from the dock or end card, ⋯ Delete, Back to Unread, Speed read's Archive) reopens that article in the Reader at its saved position when undone. List Undo puts the row back in place. After Undo on the share sheet, the sheet reads 'Removed from Shelf' for 1.5 s, then returns to the host app.
- **Each kind of wait has one pattern.** Another app or device (nostr sign-in, switch, delete approval): the centred waiting block, a secondary Cancel in the action bar, a ← that does what Cancel does, and a declined outcome. The declined outcome returns to the step that started the wait with an errline ('The request was declined in your nostr app. Nothing has changed.' / '…Nothing has been deleted.'). Shelf's own network step with the input still on screen (code check, browser Connect): shown in the pressed button. Counted work: progress card. Multi-party deletion: steps list.
- **Errors appear where the problem is.** Errors in typed input appear at the field (.codefield.bad or .wf.bad plus .fielderr). Outcome errors (expired, declined, nothing found, can't connect) go in the notice slot under the h1. Two exceptions stay as they are: dev-expired's errline under its QR, and delete-partial's errline under the steps list.
- **Offline is said once per screen.** It goes in the notice slot: 'Offline · showing saved copies' where content still works, and '[Action] needs a connection' where it blocks something. Blocked controls are disabled and don't repeat the reason. On You there is no banner; the rows carry the reason, with 'Needs a connection' on Add another device and on Use my nostr app instead. The wording is always 'when you're online'.
- **Motion uses one enter and one exit, whatever the cause.** --motion-enter is 250 ms cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1): translateY(24px) plus fade for the toast and mini, translateY(100%) for sheets, and a fade for the scrim. --motion-exit is 200 ms cubic-bezier(.4,0,1,1), the exact reverse, on every removal path. The Reader strip, if kept, slides from under the top bar and reverses. List rows collapse over 200 ms on archive, restore or delete and expand again on Undo. The toast and Archive move to new stack offsets on the enter curve. Persistent chrome does not replay on screen changes (Q3). With reduced motion, everything uses opacity only at the same durations.
- **Pairing uses one template.** The explain screen shows an illustration of the other device displaying its code, one line on how the camera is used, a primary 'Use the camera', and a no-camera escape, all before any permission prompt. Then scan. The device holding the shelf authorises: browsers get 'Connect this browser?' with the browser's name, and for phones, showing the QR is the authorisation. If the camera is refused: primary = the way forward without the camera, secondary 'Open Android settings'. A browser's XXXX-XXXX code is typed in its own step (so the keyboard never covers a live viewfinder). The phone case is Q9.
- **End-of-article cards: the primary changes state, the secondary moves on.** In both the Reader and Speed read, the primary is Archive, or Back to Unread for an archived article (decision 37). The secondary is 'Next unread' in the Reader (label is Q10) and 'Back to article' in Speed read. Pairs are laid out [secondary][primary]. The floating Archive stays (decision 18).
