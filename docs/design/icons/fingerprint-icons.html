App icon candidates

Decision 67 makes the app icon a cover picked by hand from the generator and then frozen, with today’s bookmark and progress bar standing on it in ink with a paper keyline. This sheet draws 32 candidates so Richard can pick one.

Each candidate is cover v1 at depth 1 (four big shapes, or one when the square doesn’t split), drawn on Android’s 108 dp adaptive canvas from the sha256 of a seed string. The foreground is shared/brand/src/foreground.svg with its colours changed to ink with a 1.5 dp paper keyline: the read part of the bar is paper, the rest ink. The launcher shows the central 72 dp. Until one is picked, everything that needs an icon uses No. 01, the board’s own (the cover of https://shelf.app/), and marks it provisional. Nothing in shared/brand changes yet.

How one is built · No. 01, the board’s icon

Top three

Judged at the sizes people meet the icon: a 52 dp launcher icon between other apps, a 48 px extension tile and a 16 px toolbar cut. The tests were legibility at 16 px, the silhouette on light and dark wallpapers, calm against loud, and how it sits beside other apps. “Edge lost” is the share of the circle-masked icon’s outer edge that sits under 1.6:1 against the wallpaper, so lower is a firmer silhouette.

No. 01, the board’s icon and today’s provisional one, misses the three: its mint and pale-green quarters lose the edge on the light wallpaper (19%), its dark green loses it on the dark one (26%), and at 16 px its four quarters are busy round the mark.

All 32, at real size

Seeds 01 to 10 are names; 11 to 32 are shelf/icon/1 to shelf/icon/22 in order, none skipped. Icons are drawn at 1 dp = 1 CSS px: launcher icons at 52, the extension tile at 48 and the toolbar cut at 16. The themed icon is Android’s monochrome layer, today’s bookmark and bar, so it is the same for every candidate.

Seeds and hashes, for freezing

The hash is sha256 over the seed’s UTF-8 bytes exactly as written, with no normalisation (No. 01’s seed is already a normalised address, so its hash is also that address’s d tag). Computed in Node and checked again in this page. To freeze a pick, its background layer becomes ShelfCover.svg(hash, {depth: 1, size: 108}), written out once as a fixed SVG in shared/brand/src, so renaming the app or a future cover version can’t change it.
