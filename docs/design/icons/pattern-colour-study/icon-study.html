Shelf · app icon · pattern and colour

Icon study

Two choices this time: the pattern under the mark, and its colours. Every option is a real cover drawn by Shelf’s generator, cover v1, with today’s bookmark and progress bar standing on it, inked in ink or paper to suit each pair.

Pick a pattern and every colour redraws on it; pick a colour and every pattern redraws in it. The chosen pair is shown beside the pickers (above them on a phone), next to today’s icon, at real size in a browser toolbar, on home screens, in the launcher’s mask shapes and as a Play listing.

Changes with the choice: the launcher icon, the Play store icon, every extension icon (toolbar, extensions page, Web Store and the options page logo), the website’s favicon and home-screen icons, and the icon tile on the Play feature graphic and the social card; for many pairs, also how the mark is inked (with or without its ink outline, with an ink or a paper bar). Stays as it is: the shapes of the bookmark and bar, both launch screens (the mark alone, on paper or the dark theme’s background), the themed icon and notification glyph (one colour from the phone), and every save’s own cover.

How the 18 patterns were chosen

  • 566 patterns came from the generator: seeds shelf/icon/1 to /600 and the first round’s 32, duplicates removed; a few still draw the same, such as a ring turned.
  • Three judges shortlisted them separately: how the mark sits, character, and range. Today’s bands, the hill and the ring were picked by all three.
  • Between them they picked 30. The 18 here are every pattern two or three judges picked, and six more that cover kinds the rest don’t; pairs of round lobes were left out, since one judge found they can read as anatomy.
  • The mark covers nearly half the visible circle, all of its middle, so a pattern shows mostly in the ring around it. Patterns with two or three big zones hold up; busy ones turn to noise at 16 px.
  • Single discs, quarters and lenses were dropped: under the crop a disc leaves a flat tile, and a quarter or lens only a hairline sliver at the rim.

Colours

Drawn on the pattern chosen above. A palette has four colours: the ground, one loud colour, which every cover uses at least once, and two supporting colours (on a light ground one dark and one pale; on a dark ground one pale and one mid-tone). One-shape patterns use only the loud colour and the ground, except Hill and sky, whose sky is a supporting colour. Four-shape patterns can cover most of the ground, so a palette’s name describes the palette, not always what shows.

The choice is frozen under its hash as static artwork in shared/brand/src, so the icon stays a cover drawn by cover v1 (decision 67) and never changes with the generator or a rename. A new flow decision replaces decision 70 and the parts of 72 that tune today’s cuts. The brand files are rebuilt with build-icons.cjs; if the chosen mark draws the bookmark or the bar in paper alone, the light launch screen keeps today’s mark as its own source file, since paper vanishes on a paper background. The 16, 28, 32 and 48 px cuts are tuned by hand. The small cuts on this page are drawn straight from the cover, so today’s icon looks slightly different here from its hand-tuned files. The mark for each pair was chosen by sampling the colours just outside the bookmark and the bar, where the mark meets the pattern; pairs where part of it still fades say so.
