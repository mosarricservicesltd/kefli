No. 12, in other colours

Richard liked candidate No. 12 and asked to see it in other colour sets. At icon size No. 12 is one shape, bands on a ground, so only two of its four inks show: the ground and the signal. In cover v1 the first four bytes of the hash choose the colours (the hue in bytes 0 and 1, the scheme in byte 2, a light or dark ground in byte 3) and bytes 4 to 19 choose the shapes. Every icon on this page keeps No. 12’s shape bytes and changes only the colour bytes, so each one is still a genuine cover v1 output with exactly No. 12’s shape.

The signal’s colour follows the hue. The ground follows the hue too, turned by the scheme (analogous −34°, complement +26°, triad +240°, split +210°), and is either dark or light. No. 12 itself is triad, dark ground, 63°: orange bands on aubergine. Each variant has a code: scheme (A analogous, C complement, T triad, S split), ground (D dark, L light) and hue in degrees, so No. 12 is T·D·063.

Seeds and hashes, for freezing

Each variant’s hash is No. 12’s hash (sha256 of “shelf/icon/2”) with its first four bytes replaced: two for the hue, one for the scheme (its lowest two bits), one for the ground (0C dark, C0 light). No. 12 itself keeps its real hash. Freeze whichever is picked under this exact hash.
