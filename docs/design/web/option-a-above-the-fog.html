How it works

Everything you meant to read, kept.

Save

Save from anywhere

Share a link from any app on your phone, or click the extension in Chrome or Firefox. Kefli fetches the article and keeps its text.

Read

Read it clean

Just the words and pictures, set for reading, without the clutter around them. It's all on your phone, so it reads the same on a train with no signal.

Listen

Listen on the go

Any article can be read aloud in your phone's own voice. Lock the screen and keep listening, with the controls on the lock screen.

Kept

Your library outlives us.

A read-it-later app is only as good as its promise to keep what you give it. Kefli is built so that promise doesn't depend on us.

Encrypted on your phone

Your saves are encrypted with your own key before they leave your phone, so we can't read what you've saved.

Copied beyond our servers

By default your library is also copied to servers we don't run. If Kefli closed tomorrow, your library would still be there.

No signup

Start a library and write down your recovery code. There's no email address, no password and no profile. No feed, no tags, no social layer either.

Open source

The apps are published under the AGPL, so anyone can read what they do and keep them running.

kefli
Said KEF-lee
The name

Kefli is Old Norse for a short wooden stick. Rune sticks, rúnakefli, were everyday notes and labels carved into wood so they could be carried and kept.

Many are still legible after centuries in the ground. A note made to outlast the hand that carved it is what we want your library to be.
