WP 02 · Balestrand, Sognefjorden61.2070° N 6.5340° E

Save it now. Read it in the quiet.

  • Save

    Share any link from your phone, or click the extension in Chrome or Firefox. Kefli fetches the article and keeps a clean copy.

  • Read

    The article's text and pictures, set for reading, without the clutter around them. A copy lives on your phone, so it opens offline.

  • Listen

    Any article can be read aloud in your phone's own voice, with controls on the lock screen. Good for the walk, the washing-up and the drive.

No signup. Start a library, keep your recovery code, and you're reading. No feed, no tags, no social layer.

WP 03 · Solund, mouth of Sognefjorden61.08° N 4.84° E

Your library outlives us.

  • Encrypted on your phone

    Your saves are encrypted on your phone before they leave it. We can't read what you keep.

  • Copied beyond our servers

    By default your library is also copied to servers we don't run. If Kefli closed tomorrow, your library would still be there.

  • Open source

    The apps are open source under the AGPL, so anyone can read the code and check these claims.

WP 04 · Bryggen, Bergen60.3975° N 5.3240° E

A short wooden stick.

In Old Norse, a kefli is a short stick of wood. Rune sticks, rúnakefli, were everyday notes carved into wood so they could be carried and kept. Many have been found at Bryggen, the old wharf in Bergen.

We named the app after them, as a place for the things you mean to keep.

Deyr fé, deyja frændr … en orðstírr deyr aldregi.

Cattle die, kinsmen die … but the good name never dies.

Hávamál
