    How it works

    Save it, read it, listen to it.

    Kefli does the three things a read-it-later app should do, and keeps everything you save in one library on your phone.

    Save

    Share any link to Kefli from your phone, or click the extension in Chrome or Firefox. It's in your library a moment later.

    Read

    Articles open clean, in a calm reading typeface, without the ads and pop-ups. Everything you save is there offline.

    Listen

    Kefli reads any article aloud in your phone's own voice, with controls on the lock screen.

    No signup · No feed · No tags · No social layer

    Kept

    If Kefli closed tomorrow, your library would still be there.

    Your saves belong to you, under a key only you hold. We built Kefli so that nothing you keep depends on us staying in business.

    01Encrypted on your phone

    Your saves are encrypted on your phone before they leave it, so we can't read what you've saved.

    02Copied beyond us

    By default your library is also copied to servers we don't run. It doesn't rely on ours.

    03No account to lose

    There's no signup. You start a library and keep its recovery code, and that code brings your library back on a new phone.

    04Open source

    The apps are open source under the AGPL, so anyone can read the code and check what it does.

    The name

    A stick for carrying words.

    Kefli is Old Norse for a short wooden stick. Rune sticks, rúnakefli, were everyday notes carved into wood so they could be carried and kept.

    Kefli is the same idea for what you read. It's said KEF-lee.

    deyr fé, deyja frændr … en orðstírr deyr aldregi
    Cattle die, kinsmen die … but the good name never dies. From the Hávamál.
