Saves card

The first component, finished before anything else is built: one Saves row in every state it can be in, light and dark. In Fingerprint the card becomes a catalogue row, flat on the page with a hairline under it and the article’s cover at 64 px on the right. Cover v1 draws it from the sha256 of the article’s normalised address, so the same save wears the same cover on every phone, in the extension and after a restore. Photos never appear in a row; they wait in the Reader, as published.

Tapping anywhere on the row opens the Reader; swipe left archives. Archived rows add a ⋯ that opens a bottom sheet with Back to Unread and Delete. Delete has no confirmation, just five seconds to undo. Reading progress runs along the row’s hairline in the article’s own ink, with time left in place of the total.
