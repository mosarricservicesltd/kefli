Saves card

The first component, finished before anything else is built. One card, every state it can be in, light and dark. Tapping anywhere on the card opens the Reader; swipe left archives. Archived cards add a ⋯ button with Back to Unread and Delete; Delete has no confirmation, just five seconds to undo. Reading progress is the signature: a thin Terracotta bar along the bottom and time left in place of the total.
