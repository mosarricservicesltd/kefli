Player: one listening session, three sizes. The mini player sits at the bottom of every screen (8 px above the tab bar, or 16 px above the screen edge). In the playing article’s Reader it expands in place into the full row: play, time, ±15 s, speed, and ✕ once paused. Its line replaces the reading line. On Listen the Now playing card is the player; the mini slides in once the card has scrolled mostly away. Only play controls play: a Listen row’s body opens the article, its round button plays.

Bottom stack: tab bar or screen edge, then the player, then the screen’s own bar or floating Archive, then the toast, 8 px apart. Back from a Reader returns to whatever opened it.

Build notes: Android Back is swallowed while switching accounts moves saves and while a deletion runs. Mark the recovery-code clip as sensitive (ClipDescription.EXTRA_IS_SENSITIVE); on Android 13+ the system confirms copies, so Shelf shows its own copy toast only below API 33. Toasts use getRecommendedTimeoutMillis(5000) and never auto-dismiss while a screen reader is on. In a Custom Tab, Archive is the tab’s action button and Copy link and Delete are menu items.
