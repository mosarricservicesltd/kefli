Look (Fingerprint, decisions 65–70): every save wears its own cover, drawn by cover v1 from the sha256 of its normalised address, so the same article has the same face on every surface. The chrome is ink, paper, two greys and hairlines; the article lends its ink to its progress line, link underline and spoken-word highlight, and Night paper is tinted per article. A row draws its cover once, when the save lands. The app icon in the share row is C·L·063 with the paper mark (decision 70), frozen artwork from shared/brand/src. The lock screen’s media card shows the notification glyph in its app slot, the one-colour bookmark and bar from monochrome.svg, in the system’s tint.

Player: one listening session, three sizes. The mini player sits at the bottom of every screen (8 px above the tab bar, or 16 px above the screen edge). In the playing article’s Reader it expands in place into the full row: play, time, ±15 s, speed, and ✕ once paused. Its line replaces the reading line. On Listen the Now playing card is the player; the mini slides in once the card has scrolled mostly away. Only play controls play: a Listen row’s body opens the article, its round button plays.

Bottom stack: tab bar or screen edge, then the player, then the screen’s own bar or floating Archive, then the toast, 8 px apart. Back from a Reader returns to whatever opened it.

Motion (decision 73, motion-spec.md): everything fades by default and travels only to show where something went, on the motion tokens, and only transform and opacity animate. A cover draws once, when its save lands in front of you (the share sheet, “A save arrives from Chrome”, a restore, “…the first save lands”). Each article’s line is carried at its place: open a part-read row, read on, and come back to see it catch up; Archive from the Reader to see it finish before the row leaves. With reduced motion every meaning stays and the travel goes. Jumps land in place.

Build notes: Android Back is swallowed while switching accounts moves saves and while a deletion runs. Mark the recovery-code clip as sensitive (ClipDescription.EXTRA_IS_SENSITIVE); on Android 13+ the system confirms copies, so Shelf shows its own copy toast only below API 33. Toasts use getRecommendedTimeoutMillis(5000) and never auto-dismiss while a screen reader is on. In a Custom Tab, Archive is the tab’s action button and Copy link and Delete are menu items.
