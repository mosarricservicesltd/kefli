Shelf prototype · batch 5 · browser extension

Click the Shelf button in the toolbar (the terracotta bookmark) or press Alt+Shift+S to save the page, or jump to any state below. The toast pauses while the pointer is over it or focus is inside it; Escape or ✕ dismisses it. Shelf’s options open in their own tab; click the article’s tab to go back. The browser window, tab strip and right-click menu are Chrome’s, drawn only for context; the toast and the options page are Shelf’s.

One change from the extension spec for you to decide: the spec drops the oldest queued save when a ninth is made offline. Here the ninth is refused instead, with a clear message, so nothing you saved is ever silently lost.
