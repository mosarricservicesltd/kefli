Design study · covers · 9 October 2026 · not a decision

A winter palette

Every cover here is drawn by cover v1 itself (shared/cover/cover-v1.js): the same layout, leaves, rotations and seams from the same hash. Only the four colours change. Current v1 sits beside three cooler directions, Fjord, Frost & heather and Slate, on the same 28 saves, in the Saves list on paper and on graphite, and in the Night reader, whose paper, line and highlight also come from the palette.

01The rules

Each option maps the hue bytes (bytes 0 and 2, a 16-bit value) linearly into its range, so saves spread as evenly across the range as v1 spreads them round the wheel. The scheme (byte 4) and the dark ground (byte 6 below 80, about three covers in ten) are kept. Lightness is v1's throughout; only chroma is scaled. Ranges are OKLCH hue in degrees.

02Covers

The same 28 hashes in each column: the 12 saves in the phone prototype first, then 16 more addresses hashed for this study.

03The Saves list

Rows as the app draws them, with the cover on the right and the reading progress in the article's line ink. Paper (#F3F2EE) above, graphite (#111112) below.

04The Night reader

Night reads each article on its own dark paper tinted with the cover's hue; the progress line and the spoken-word highlight are the cover's signal colour, checked for contrast as cover v1 checks them (3:1 for the line, 7:1 for text on the highlight).
